Claiming the big one 🧾

Official HKJC procedure. Rules and hotline numbers change — always confirm the current details on hkjc.com before acting.

The 60-day rule

A valid winning ticket must be presented for payment within 60 days of the day after the draw. After that the prize is forfeited. Claims are made on business days only.

Where to collect, by amount

Up to HK$1,000,000 (incl. 4th–7th)Any off-course betting branch or racecourse
Over HK$1,000,000HKJC Headquarters, 1 Sports Road, Happy Valley
1st / 2nd division over HK$5,000,000Register on the HKJC prize hotline within the 60 days, then collect at HKJC Headquarters

Betting-account bets

Winnings up to HK$5,000,000 are credited to the account automatically. A 1st or 2nd division win over HK$5,000,000 still needs hotline registration within 60 days.

Bring with you

  • The original winning ticket — it is a bearer instrument. Keep it flat and undamaged; a lost or destroyed ticket cannot be replaced. Photograph it.
  • Your HKID card or passport and a contact address. Large prizes are normally paid by cheque.
